

# Ferry Hoes Personal Brand Website
*A creative thought leader HQ – not a marketing site*

---

## Phase 1: Homepage & Visual Foundation

### Design System
- **Typography**: Bold condensed headlines (Bebas Neue) + clean readable body text
- **Colors**: Deep blue (#0076cd), white, black, soft greys with subtle accent gradients
- **Style**: Magazine-like editorial feel with organic layouts, rounded shapes, lots of whitespace
- **Motion**: Subtle hover effects and micro-animations throughout

### Homepage Sections (in order)

1. **Hero** – Full-impact opening
   - Giant headline: "REBUILDING THE WAY THE WORLD WORKS"
   - Conversational subtext about exploring human + technology intersection
   - Two CTAs: "Start reading" + "Join the community (coming soon)"

2. **Intro Block** – "This is my home base"
   - Warm, conversational text explaining this is a lab, not a funnel
   - Organic layout with personality

3. **Content Grid** – Latest writings
   - 3-6 editorial-style blog cards with placeholder content
   - Clean, magazine-inspired design
   - "View all writing" button

4. **Podcast Section** – Featured audio
   - Large visual treatment
   - Brief description + "Listen to the podcast" button

5. **Video Section** – Visual content showcase
   - Thumbnail grid layout
   - "Watch videos" button

6. **Speaking** – Soft introduction
   - Text-focused section about Ferry's speaking work
   - "About my speaking" button linking to full page

7. **Community** – Emotional call to connect
   - Big headline: "Don't follow. Build with me."
   - Placeholder for future Slack waitlist signup

8. **Mini About** – Personal introduction
   - Friendly, approachable block about Ferry

9. **Footer** – Minimal & memorable
   - Essential links + tagline: "Humans first. Technology second. Always."
