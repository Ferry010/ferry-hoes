# Ferry Hoes

Build a modern, content-first personal brand website for a thought leader and international speaker named Ferry Hoes.




The site should feel:
	•	human
	•	conversational
	•	editorial / magazine-like
	•	creative
	•	non-corporate
	•	playful but intelligent
	•	NOT salesy




Think:
Wired magazine × personal blog × clubhouse × creative lab




STYLE
	•	Big bold typography (Bebas Neue or similar condensed headline font)
	•	Huge titles
	•	Lots of whitespace
	•	Rounded shapes
	•	Subtle gradients
	•	Soft shadows
	•	Organic layout (not strict boxes)
	•	Warm, human photography style
	•	Slight motion/hover effects
	•	Playful details
	•	Color palette: deep blue (#0076cd), white, black, soft greys + subtle accent gradients
	•	No corporate stock imagery
	•	Feels alive and personal




STRUCTURE




Create these homepage sections in this order:




1 HERO
Big headline:
“REBUILDING THE WAY THE WORLD WORKS”




Subtext:
“I explore how humans and technology actually work better together. Through stories, experiments, talks and conversations.”




Buttons:
	•	Start reading
	•	Join the community (Slack – coming soon)




2 INTRO – What this place is
Headline: “This is my home base.”
Short conversational paragraph explaining it’s a lab, not a funnel.




3 CONTENT GRID
Dynamic blog cards (latest posts)
3–6 cards
Editorial look
Button: View all writing




4 PODCAST SECTION
Large visual
Short text
Button: Listen to the podcast




5 VIDEO SECTION
Video thumbnails grid
Button: Watch videos




6 SPEAKING (soft sell)
Text-focused section about speaking
Button: About my speaking page




7 COMMUNITY
Big emotional section
Headline:
“Don’t follow. Build with me.”
Slack community waitlist signup




8 MINI ABOUT
Small friendly block introducing Ferry




9 FOOTER
Minimal
Links + tagline:
“Humans first. Technology second. Always.”




FUNCTIONALITY
	•	CMS for blog
	•	CMS for podcast episodes
	•	CMS for videos
	•	Easy card layout
	•	Smooth scrolling
	•	Micro animations
	•	Mobile first
	•	Fast load
	•	Slack waitlist email capture
	•	Separate speaking page
	•	Contact form




TONE OF COPY
Conversational
Short sentences
Human
Smart but accessible
No corporate jargon




Make it feel like a creative thought leader HQ, not a marketing website.
